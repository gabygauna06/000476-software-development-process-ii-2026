This section contains theoretical material used during the course, organized according to the official subject structure.

## Organization Criterion

Theoretical material is organized by official course unit whenever the relationship with the syllabus is clearly established.

New unit directories are created only when actual course material becomes available.

Files in this section may include:

- class presentations
- theoretical handouts
- conceptual material
- diagrams and modeling material used in class

Practical activities, official administrative documents, examinations, and external references belong in their corresponding sections.
