# Unit 01 — Design

Practical work related to the initial Software Design unit of Software Development Process II.

[Versión en español](README.es.md)

## Purpose

This unit groups practical activities focused on identifying and analyzing software design problems, proposed solutions, design decisions, and software quality attributes.

It complements the theoretical material developed during the first course unit.

## Current Structure

- `activity-01-paper-analysis/`: analysis of a scientific paper from the CONAIISI 2024 proceedings

## Current Activities

### Activity 01 — Paper Analysis

The first activity requires reading an assigned scientific paper and identifying:

1. the problem addressed
2. the proposed solution
3. the design decisions involved
4. the quality attributes the solution attempts to improve

## Organization Criterion

Activities belonging to this unit are stored in independent directories so that their instructions, solutions, source projects, and final deliverables remain clearly separated and traceable.

Additional activities will be incorporated only when they are assigned by the course.
